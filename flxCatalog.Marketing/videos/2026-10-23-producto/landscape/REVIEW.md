# Review · flxCatalog · un pedido, a dos lados

Covers both formats (`landscape/`, `vertical/`). Spec: `../composition-brief.md`. Story: `../video-plan.md`.

## Creative calls

- Storyline: the user picked "A y B" — read as one mixed storyline (one real cart of 139,70 €, told on both sides).
  Not built: B alone (split screen for the whole film) and C (a one-feature-per-scene tour).
- One carrier: the cart (Malla Deportiva Prime x2 + Pantalón Cargo Nova) travels card → dialog → cart → admin board.
- Accent rationed to the CARR card ring, the Entregado ring and Flexygo's own logo.
- Transitions: zoom through a card, button-to-dialog morph, whip, carry across, blur swaps on one ground.

## Fixed in review

- P0 hook title broke onto three lines → 92 px, no wrap.
- P0 the "Añadir al carrito" label was hidden under the morph shape from frame 0 → shape held hidden until the click.
- P1 cart summary cut "Dirección de entrega" → wider crop at 1.9x, the travelling total recomputed.
- P1 tracking bar too thin to read → 3.2x with a camera that follows it (landscape).
- P1 the pulled-back board read as grey noise → the dashboard's own charts under the KPI.
- P1 vertical: a sliver of the previous kanban card showed and the ring sat high → crop and ring moved.

## Measured vs looked at

- `hyperframes check`: 0 errors, 0 warnings in both formats (after fixes).
- Looked at: beat frames and every seam (landscape), beat frames (vertical).
- Sound: placed, not heard. Music entered at 15 s of the track so cuts land on its 120 BPM grid.

## Weakest shot

Shot 5 (tracking): the real bar is small UI, so it is shown big and partly veiled; it is the shot that reads
least like "the product doing its job". With more time: cut from the "Sí" click straight onto the admin board
with the card in Confirmado — which needs a confirmed order on screen, i.e. writing data, not done.
